## 2. Information lifecycle

Document each information category, purpose, source, collection point, user, storage location, recipients, retention period and disposal method. Include client identifiers, contact details, assessment responses, safety information, clinician notes, reports, audit events and technical metadata as applicable.

## 3. Purpose, authority and transparency

- State each specific purpose and lawful basis for collection and use.
- Identify the responsible health agency and service relationship.
- Record the collection notice shown at or before collection.
- Explain voluntary/mandatory fields and consequences of not providing information.
- Record consent or other applicable authority and its version.
- Explain access, correction and complaint pathways.

## 4. Security and access

Assess authentication, MFA, role-based access, least privilege, tenant separation, encryption in transit/at rest, key management, backups, audit logging, administrator access, endpoint security, vulnerability management and incident response.

## 5. Disclosure and hosting

List hosting providers, locations, subprocessors, support access, overseas disclosures, integrations, analytics and backups. Record contracts, data processing terms and cross-border safeguards.

## 6. Retention and disposal

Define record classes, retention periods, legal/clinical holds, deletion workflows, backup expiry, archival, secure destruction and evidence of disposal.

## 7. Individual rights and safety

Document identity verification, access and correction workflows, disclosure logging, handling of minors or representatives, urgent safety disclosures, complaints and escalation.
