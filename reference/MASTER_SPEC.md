# STN Clinical Follow-Up & Diagnostic Formulation Assessment — Master Specification v1

## Purpose
This is a second-stage assessment opened after the Initial Assessment when a clinician needs more information to clarify diagnostic possibilities, risk, functional impact, treatment needs or referral level.

It should combine the STN Whole Person Initial Assessment, approved clinical core and governed diagnostic-domain modules while separating screening, formulation and diagnosis.

## Distinction from Initial Assessment
- clinician-led rather than self-screening only;
- imports the Initial Assessment instead of asking everything again;
- full chronology, history, medical/substance/developmental differential and collateral needs;
- structured Mental State Examination;
- structured diagnostic-domain evidence review;
- validated measures only where authorised;
- integrated 5Ps + Te Whare Tapa Whā formulation;
- explicit rule-outs and uncertainty;
- diagnostic indication status rather than automated diagnosis;
- signed care plan and auditable finalisation.

## Diagnostic indication statuses
- Not assessed
- Not supported by current evidence
- Possible — requires more information
- Supported for clinician consideration
- Rule-out / alternative explanation under review
- Refer for specialist assessment

A formal diagnosis is recorded only when an appropriately authorised clinician makes it within scope.

## Output
1. Safety status and safety actions.
2. Presenting problem and chronology.
3. MSE summary.
4. Validated measure results.
5. Domain evidence table.
6. Differential diagnosis and exclusions.
7. Integrated formulation.
8. Diagnostic indication(s) and uncertainty.
9. Treatment/referral/wrap-around plan.
10. Review interval, outcome measures and clinician sign-off.
