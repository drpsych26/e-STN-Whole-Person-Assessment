# STN intellectual-property ownership and assignment checklist

**Status: ownership transfer NOT VERIFIED.** This is an action checklist, not a signed assignment or legal opinion.

## Intended position
The intended commercial owner is Supportive Therapy Network Limited (STN Limited). The intended assets may include original STN application source code, original UI/UX, workflow designs, documentation, branding and original written materials, only to the extent that the rights holder owns them and they are legally assignable.

A founder and a limited company are separate legal persons. A copyright notice does not itself transfer rights to the company. Ownership depends on who created each asset, employment/contract terms, third-party contributions, pre-existing works and signed assignments already in place.

## Required asset register
For each asset record its name/path/version/hash, creator(s), creation date, current owner, evidence of ownership, contractor/employee contributions, third-party dependencies, instrument/questionnaire licensing terms, assignment/licence reference, status and reviewer.

## Actions
1. Verify STN Limited's exact registered legal name and company number from the NZ Companies Register.
2. Identify the actual rights holder for every HTML prototype, Flutter package, paper assessment, logo, document, source file and contribution.
3. Separate original STN content from third-party instruments, questionnaire text, libraries, fonts, icons and templates. STN cannot assign rights it does not own.
4. Have a New Zealand IP lawyer prepare/review a written assignment deed and any confirmatory licences. The deed should identify the works and rights, parties, effective date, consideration where relevant, warranties/limitations, further assurances and governing law.
5. Obtain signatures from every necessary rights holder and retain signed originals. Update the asset register.
6. Use a company copyright notice only on works whose ownership has been verified, e.g. © 2026 Supportive Therapy Network Limited. All rights reserved.
7. Keep the GitHub repository private; enable MFA, least-privilege access, protected branches, secret scanning, dependency/licence scanning and independent backups.

Private repository settings reduce access but do not prove ownership or prevent all copying. Third-party content remains subject to its own rights and licence terms.
