Client details and consent

Informed consent

Draft consent wording — requires STN clinical and legal approval before real-client use. Explain this information in an accessible way, invite questions, check understanding and record the client's own decision. Do not pressure the client to agree.

Purpose: This assessment gathers information about the client's wellbeing, needs, strengths, functioning and safety to support discussion and care planning. Screening responses are not, by themselves, a diagnosis or a complete risk assessment.

Information handling: Explain what information is collected, why it is needed, who in the care team may access it, when information may need to be shared for safety or another lawful purpose, how the client can request access or correction, and the applicable retention and privacy arrangements. Give the client the approved STN privacy notice before asking for consent.

Choice and limits: Participation is voluntary. The client may ask questions, pause, decline optional information-sharing, or withdraw consent. Explain any practical or legal limits on confidentiality before proceeding. Declining optional sharing must not be treated as refusal of the assessment.

Optional information-sharing choices

These choices are separate from consent to this assessment. Leave unchecked unless the client gives specific, informed permission.

Whole-person screening

Demonstration items only. Rate 0–4 based on the assessment conversation. These are not validated instruments.

Safety gate

Clinical review required: this prototype does not determine emergency disposition or replace a real-time risk assessment. If there is immediate danger, follow local emergency and organisational procedures.
