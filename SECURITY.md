# Security and clinical safety

- Never use real identifiable client data in this prototype or test repository.
- Do not store secrets in source control.
- Keep this repository private while the content and clinical governance remain under review.
- Prototype scoring and safety flags are demonstrative only and are not validated clinical rules.
- Production deployment requires formal clinical governance, instrument licensing review, privacy/security assessment, penetration testing, backup/recovery planning, and clinician-approved escalation procedures.
